# About KITM

## Overview
Kaveri Institute of Technology and Management (KITM) is a private engineering and management college in North Bengaluru, established in 2004. It is approved by AICTE, affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), and holds a NAAC A grade accreditation. The campus is spread over 45 acres and has about 4,800 students. (This is a fictional college used for demonstration.)

## Programmes offered
Undergraduate B.E. programmes: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), CSE with AI and Machine Learning, Information Science and Engineering (ISE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), Mechanical Engineering and Civil Engineering. Postgraduate programmes: M.Tech in VLSI Design, M.Tech in Computer Science, and MBA. The college also has a PhD research centre in Computer Science and Electronics.
