# Canteen and Food

## Canteens on campus
KITM has three food outlets: the Main Canteen near the library, the Food Court next to the sports complex, and the Juice and Coffee Corner at the administrative block. The Main Canteen is open from 8 AM to 5:30 PM and the Food Court from 11 AM to 8 PM. The Juice and Coffee Corner is open from 8 AM to 6 PM. Parents on campus visits can eat at the Main Canteen. All canteens are hygienic, inspected every month and have food safety certification.

## Menu and prices
Breakfast items include idli, dosa, vada, poori and upma at Rs. 30 to Rs. 50. Lunch is available as a meals thali for Rs. 70, with a South Indian vegetarian meal and North Indian options. The Food Court serves biryani for Rs. 120, chicken rolls for Rs. 70, noodles for Rs. 80, pizza for Rs. 100, and sandwiches and burgers from Rs. 50. Tea and coffee cost Rs. 12 to Rs. 20. Fresh juices cost Rs. 30 to Rs. 50, and ice cream and shakes are also available.

## Food types and payment
The Main Canteen is purely vegetarian, and the Food Court has both vegetarian and non-vegetarian counters kept separate. Jain food and Diwali, Eid and Christmas special menus are provided on request. Payments can be made by UPI, card or cash. Students can also use the KITM Connect app wallet for a 5 percent discount.

## Drinking water
Free filtered drinking water points are available in every block and canteen.
