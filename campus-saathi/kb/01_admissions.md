# Admissions

## Eligibility
For B.E. programmes a student must have passed Class 12 (PUC or equivalent) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects and Chemistry, Computer Science, Biotechnology or Electronics as the third subject, with at least 45 percent marks overall (40 percent for reserved categories). For M.Tech a valid B.E. or B.Tech degree and a GATE or PGCET rank is needed. For MBA a bachelor's degree with 50 percent and a valid KMAT, CMAT or PGCET score is needed.

## Seats and quotas
The B.E. intake is 1,080 seats in total: CSE 240, CSE with AI and ML 180, ISE 180, ECE 180, EEE 90, Mechanical 120 and Civil 90. Seats are filled through three routes: Karnataka government quota via KCET counselling, COMEDK UGET counselling, and management quota. Management quota applications are made directly at the college admission office.

## Admission process and important dates
Applications for 2026 opened in January and the management quota application closes at the end of July. KCET and COMEDK students are admitted through their counselling rounds. Steps: register online or at the admission office, submit documents, attend a short counselling session, pay the first instalment of fees, and receive the admission letter.

## Documents required
Class 10 and Class 12 marks cards, transfer certificate, KCET or COMEDK rank card and allotment letter, caste and income certificate if applicable, six passport size photographs, Aadhaar card copy, and a migration certificate for students from other boards.

## Previous cutoffs
Last year closing KCET ranks were roughly 9,000 for CSE, 14,000 for CSE with AI and ML, 17,000 for ISE, 26,000 for ECE, 42,000 for EEE, 58,000 for Mechanical and 75,000 for Civil.
