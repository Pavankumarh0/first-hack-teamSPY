# Parking

## Visitor parking
Parents and visitors can park free of cost in the visitor parking area near the main gate, which has space for 120 cars and 200 two-wheelers. The guard at the gate will give directions. During admission counselling days and events, extra parking is arranged on the sports ground.

## Student parking
Students may bring two-wheelers and cars after registering for a parking pass at the security office. The parking pass costs Rs. 1,500 per year for two-wheelers and Rs. 4,000 per year for four-wheelers. Student parking has a capacity of 1,500 two-wheelers and 250 cars, and is in the south parking yard behind the sports complex. First year students are allowed to bring vehicles only with a valid driving licence and a parent's consent letter.

## Rules and facilities
Helmets are compulsory for two-wheeler riders. The speed limit inside campus is 20 kilometres per hour. Parking yards are covered by CCTV and a security guard is on duty 24 hours. There are six electric vehicle charging points near the administrative block at Rs. 12 per unit. Bicycles can be parked free at cycle stands near each academic block. Parking is open from 7 AM to 8 PM, and vehicles cannot stay overnight except for hostel residents with special permission.
