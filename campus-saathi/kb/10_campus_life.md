# Campus Life, Scholarships and Other Information

## Clubs and events
There are more than 40 student clubs, including coding club, robotics club, entrepreneurship cell, photography, dance, music, drama, literary, and a Kannada Sangha. The annual technical fest Technovate takes place in March, and the cultural fest Utsav is held in October. Students also take part in a national hackathon every year.

## Scholarships and financial help
Merit scholarships up to 50 percent of tuition are given to students with a rank within 5,000 in KCET or above 95 percent in Class 12. Government scholarships for SC, ST, OBC and minority students are available through the State Scholarship Portal. The college gives a fee waiver for children of defence personnel and for sports achievers at the state or national level.

## Rules and discipline
The dress code is smart casual, and ID cards must be worn at all times. Mobile phones are allowed but not during lab sessions. Ragging is a punishable offence with a zero tolerance policy and an anti-ragging helpline is available at 1800 4000 555.

## Student support
There is a counselling centre with a full-time psychologist, a career guidance cell, a health centre, an alumni network of over 14,000 members, and a student mentorship programme. The college also provides an Aadhaar enrolment and a bank counter on campus. International students are supported by a dedicated foreign students cell.

## Academic calendar
The odd semester begins in August and the even semester begins in February. Classes run from 9 AM to 4 PM, Monday to Friday, with a half-day on Saturday for activities.
