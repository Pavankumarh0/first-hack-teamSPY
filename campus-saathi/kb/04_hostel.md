# Hostel

## Hostel facilities
KITM has separate on-campus hostels: Ganga Bhavan for boys with 900 beds and Yamuna Bhavan for girls with 600 beds. Both are within walking distance of the academic blocks. Hostels have 24 hour security with CCTV, a resident warden, biometric entry, Wi-Fi, hot water in the mornings, laundry service, a common room with TV, an indoor games room, and a small gym. A medical room with a visiting doctor is available. Hostel is open to first year students on a priority basis.

## Room types and fees (per year)
Triple sharing room costs Rs. 70,000 per year. Double sharing room costs Rs. 95,000 per year. Single room, limited in number, costs Rs. 1,30,000 per year. Air-conditioned double rooms cost Rs. 1,25,000 per year. The fee includes electricity, water and Wi-Fi. Mess charges are Rs. 48,000 per year, and the mess serves breakfast, lunch, evening snacks and dinner.

## Mess and food
The hostel mess serves vegetarian food, and a separate non-vegetarian mess operates on weekends. Menus are decided by a student committee. Special meals are served during festivals.

## Rules and timings
Boys hostel gate closes at 10 PM and girls hostel gate closes at 8 PM on weekdays. Parents can visit on Sundays between 10 AM and 5 PM. Students can take weekend leave with a parent's written permission. Ragging is strictly prohibited. Hostel warden for girls is Mrs. Lakshmi Prasad and for boys is Mr. Ramesh Gowda. Hostel seats are allotted after admission is confirmed and a Rs. 10,000 deposit is paid.
