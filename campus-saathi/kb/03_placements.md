# Placements

## Placement statistics 2025-26
Overall 92 percent of eligible students were placed. The highest package was Rs. 42 lakh per annum, the average package was Rs. 7.8 lakh per annum and the median package was Rs. 6.5 lakh per annum. A total of 186 companies visited the campus and made over 1,150 offers. Computer Science and Information Science branches had 97 percent placement, Electronics and Electrical had 91 percent, and Mechanical and Civil had 82 percent.

## Top recruiters
Major recruiters include Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Accenture, Capgemini, Cognizant, Bosch, Siemens, Texas Instruments, Dell, Deloitte, Amazon, Goldman Sachs, Tech Mahindra and several startups. Core companies such as Bosch, L&T, Tata Motors and Toyota Kirloskar visit for Mechanical, Civil and Electrical students.

## Training and internships
The Training and Placement Cell starts aptitude, coding and communication training from the third semester. Students get a mandatory internship in the sixth or seventh semester. The college has partnerships with companies for paid internships, with stipends ranging from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 40,000 per month. Mock interviews, resume workshops, and coding contests are held every month.

## Higher studies
About 12 percent of students go for higher studies in India or abroad, and the college offers coaching for GATE, GRE and CAT. The placement head is Dr. Suresh Nambiar, and the placement office is in the Administrative Block, first floor.
