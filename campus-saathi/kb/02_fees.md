# Fee Structure

## Annual tuition fees for B.E. (academic year 2026-27)
Government quota (KCET): Rs. 85,000 per year for all branches. COMEDK quota: Rs. 1,65,000 per year for CSE, CSE with AI and ML and ISE, and Rs. 1,45,000 per year for ECE, EEE, Mechanical and Civil. Management quota: Rs. 2,40,000 per year for CSE and CSE with AI and ML, Rs. 2,10,000 for ISE and ECE, and Rs. 1,80,000 for EEE, Mechanical and Civil. Tuition fees may increase by up to 5 percent each year as per university rules.

## Other fees
One time admission and registration fee is Rs. 25,000. Refundable caution deposit is Rs. 10,000. University and examination fees are about Rs. 12,000 per year. Transport fee is separate and depends on the bus route. Hostel and mess fee is separate and is listed in the hostel information.

## Postgraduate fees
M.Tech costs Rs. 90,000 per year. MBA costs Rs. 1,20,000 per year.

## Payment options
Fees can be paid by online banking, UPI, debit or credit card, demand draft, or at the college accounts office. Tuition can be paid in two instalments per year, in August and in January, at no extra charge. A three-instalment option is available with a small processing fee of Rs. 1,500. Education loans are supported through tie-ups with SBI, Canara Bank and HDFC Credila, and the accounts office helps with the paperwork.

## Refund policy
If a student withdraws before classes begin, the full fee is refunded except Rs. 5,000 processing charge. After classes begin, no tuition fee is refunded but the caution deposit is returned at the end of the course.
