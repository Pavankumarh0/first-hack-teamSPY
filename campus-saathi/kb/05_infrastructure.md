# Infrastructure

## Campus and classrooms
The 45 acre green campus has seven academic blocks, an administrative block, a central library, an auditorium that seats 1,200 people, a seminar hall and a convocation ground. All classrooms are air-ventilated with projectors, and 60 percent are smart classrooms. The entire campus has Wi-Fi with a 1 Gbps internet link.

## Laboratories and centres
There are more than 70 laboratories, including a Cloud and AI lab with GPU servers, an IoT and embedded systems lab, a VLSI design lab, a robotics lab, CAD and CAM labs, a 3D printing lab and structural testing labs for Civil. The college also has an Innovation and Incubation Centre where students can build startups, and a Centre of Excellence with Bosch for automotive electronics.

## Library
The central library is open from 8 AM to 9 PM on working days and until 5 PM on Sundays during exams. It has over 62,000 books, subscriptions to IEEE, Springer and Elsevier journals, a digital library with 100 computers, and quiet reading rooms that seat 400 students.

## Sports and health
Facilities include a cricket ground, football field, two basketball courts, volleyball and tennis courts, a 400 metre track, indoor badminton, table tennis and a fully equipped gym. A medical centre on campus is open 24 hours with a nurse and an ambulance. There is an ATM, a bank branch, a stationery and photocopy shop, and a campus store.

## Safety
The campus is covered by CCTV and 24 hour security. An anti-ragging committee and an internal complaints committee for women are active, and a student counsellor is available on all working days.
