# Transportation and Bus Routes

## College bus service
KITM runs 28 college buses covering Bengaluru. Buses reach the campus by 8:30 AM and leave at 4:30 PM, with a late bus at 6:30 PM for students with evening activities. Bus passes are issued at the transport office near the main gate. The annual bus fee depends on the distance and ranges from Rs. 22,000 to Rs. 38,000. All buses have GPS tracking, and the live location can be seen on the KITM Connect mobile app. Every bus has a woman attendant on girls' routes.

## Bus routes and stops
Route 1, Majestic: Majestic KSR, Mekhri Circle, Hebbal, Yelahanka New Town, Campus. Fee Rs. 26,000 per year. Pick-up at Majestic at 7:00 AM.
Route 2, Indiranagar: Indiranagar, KR Puram, Hennur Cross, Kothanur, Campus. Fee Rs. 32,000 per year. Pick-up at Indiranagar at 6:50 AM.
Route 3, Jayanagar: Jayanagar 4th Block, Basavanagudi, Malleswaram, Hebbal, Campus. Fee Rs. 38,000 per year. Pick-up at 6:30 AM.
Route 4, Whitefield: Whitefield, Hoodi, Marathahalli Bridge, Hennur, Campus. Fee Rs. 38,000 per year. Pick-up at 6:30 AM.
Route 5, Rajajinagar: Rajajinagar, Yeshwantpur, Jalahalli, Yelahanka, Campus. Fee Rs. 24,000 per year. Pick-up at 7:10 AM.
Route 6, Airport Road: Sahakar Nagar, Jakkur, Bagalur Cross, Campus. Fee Rs. 22,000 per year. Pick-up at 7:30 AM.

## Public transport
BMTC city buses 298 and 290 stop at Devanahalli Road, in front of the college gate. The nearest railway station is Yelahanka Junction, 3 km away, and an auto rickshaw costs about Rs. 60. The nearest metro station is Yelahanka, planned on the new line, and until then students use the BMTC feeder buses. Kempegowda International Airport is 12 km from campus, which takes about 25 minutes by cab.
