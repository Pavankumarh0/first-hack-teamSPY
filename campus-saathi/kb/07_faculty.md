# Teaching Staff

## Faculty strength and qualifications
KITM has 320 full-time faculty members, and 62 percent hold a PhD. The student to teacher ratio is 15 to 1. Many faculty members have industry experience, and 40 visiting professors from IISc, IIT and industry teach special courses every year. Faculty have published more than 400 research papers in the last three years and hold 18 patents.

## Principal and department heads
The Principal is Dr. Anand Kulkarni, who holds a PhD from IISc and has 28 years of experience. Head of Computer Science and Engineering is Dr. Meera Raghavan. Head of Information Science is Dr. Karthik Hegde. Head of Electronics and Communication is Dr. Sunita Rao. Head of Electrical Engineering is Dr. Vinay Shetty. Head of Mechanical Engineering is Dr. Prakash Naik. Head of Civil Engineering is Dr. Farida Khan. Head of MBA is Dr. Rohit Mehta.

## Mentoring and teaching approach
Every student is assigned a faculty mentor for the full four years. Mentors meet students every week and share progress with parents through the parent portal. Classes use project based learning, with a mini project in every semester and a final year major project. Attendance of 75 percent is required to sit for exams. Remedial classes and doubt clearing sessions are held for weaker students at no cost.

## Results
The university results for the last year show an overall pass rate of 94 percent, and 38 students secured university ranks.
