# Teaching Staff and Faculty at SVIT

## Faculty strength

SVIT has 286 teaching staff members, of whom 112 hold a Ph.D. and 38 are currently
pursuing a Ph.D. The faculty includes 34 professors, 78 associate professors and 174
assistant professors. The overall student-to-faculty ratio is 17:1, and in
laboratories the ratio is maintained at one faculty member and one instructor per 30
students. The average teaching experience of senior faculty is 16 years, and 41
faculty members have prior industry experience at companies such as Bosch, Infosys,
Honeywell, Wipro and Bharat Electronics.

## Research and publications

Faculty members published 428 papers in Scopus-indexed journals and conferences over
the last three years. The college holds 22 granted patents and 61 published patent
applications. Research funding of Rs. 6.8 crore has been received from agencies
including AICTE, VGST Karnataka, DST SERB, VTU research grants and industry-sponsored
projects. There are six recognised research centres under VTU for Ph.D. supervision
in Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, Mathematics and Physics.

## Mentoring system

Every student is assigned a faculty mentor who stays the same for all four years. One
mentor handles about 20 students. Mentors meet their students formally once every
three weeks, maintain a mentoring record with academic performance and attendance, and
contact parents directly if attendance drops below 75 percent or if a student fails
more than two subjects. Parents receive an SMS and email with internal assessment
marks and attendance after every test.

## Teaching approach

Classes follow the VTU and autonomous scheme syllabus with continuous internal
evaluation. Teaching includes regular lectures, tutorial sessions, laboratory work,
mini projects every semester from the third semester, a major project in the final
year, and at least two industry guest lectures per semester per department. Faculty
use the learning management system to share notes, question banks, previous question
papers and assignment submissions. Remedial classes are conducted free of charge for
students who score below 40 percent in internal tests, held between 4:45 PM and
5:45 PM.

## Faculty availability for students and parents

Faculty are available in their department staff rooms during office hours from
9:00 AM to 4:45 PM on working days, except during their scheduled class hours. Parents
who wish to meet a faculty member or head of department are advised to call the
department office in advance. Parent-teacher meetings are held twice every semester,
usually in the fourth and twelfth week, on a Saturday.

## Faculty development

Faculty members must complete at least two one-week faculty development programmes
per year. The college sponsors conference travel up to Rs. 40,000 per paper and gives
an incentive of Rs. 15,000 for each Scopus-indexed journal publication and
Rs. 50,000 for each granted patent.
