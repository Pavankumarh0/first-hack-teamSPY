## Current fees
Checked against the official college website on 2026-10-29: the latest branch-wise fee notification located is for academic year 2024-25. These are historical figures only, not verified 2026-27 fees. The college website advertises 2026-27 admissions, but this knowledge base has not found a 2026-27 fee notification. Fees can vary by admission quota and academic year. Confirm the amount with the admissions office before making a payment.
