# Fee Structure and Scholarships at SVIT

## About the fee structure

All fees shown are per academic year in Indian Rupees for the 2025-26 session.

## B.E. tuition fee by admission route

| Admission route | Tuition fee per year |
|---|---|
| KCET government quota | Rs. 68,000 |
| KCET private quota | Rs. 1,32,000 |
| COMEDK | Rs. 2,45,000 |
| Management quota (CSE, ISE, AIML) | Rs. 4,10,000 |
| Management quota (ECE, EEE) | Rs. 3,25,000 |
| Management quota (Mechanical, Civil, Aeronautical) | Rs. 2,60,000 |
| NRI and foreign national quota | USD 5,500 |

## Additional one-time and annual charges

- One-time admission and registration fee: Rs. 12,000 (first year only)
- University affiliation and examination fee: Rs. 9,500 per year
- Laboratory and workshop fee: Rs. 8,000 per year
- Library fee: Rs. 3,000 per year
- Student activity, sports and cultural fee: Rs. 4,500 per year
- Refundable caution deposit: Rs. 10,000 (returned at the time of graduation)
- Student insurance and medical fee: Rs. 1,200 per year
- Alumni association one-time fee: Rs. 2,000 (final year)

## M.Tech and MBA fees

M.Tech tuition is Rs. 95,000 per year under PGCET quota and Rs. 1,60,000 per year
under management quota. MBA tuition is Rs. 1,45,000 per year under PGCET quota and
Rs. 2,20,000 per year under management quota.

## Payment methods and instalments

Fees can be paid by NEFT or RTGS bank transfer, through the online student portal at
fees.svit.edu.in using UPI, net banking, debit card or credit card, or by demand
draft drawn in favour of "Sri Venkateshwara Institute of Technology" payable at
Bengaluru. Cash payments above Rs. 20,000 are not accepted.

Tuition fees may be paid in two instalments. The first instalment of 60 percent is
due at the time of admission and the second instalment of 40 percent is due by
15 January of that academic year. A late payment fine of Rs. 100 per day applies
after the due date. Education loan documentation support is provided by the accounts
office, and the college has tie-ups with State Bank of India Harohalli branch,
Canara Bank and HDFC Bank for education loans.

## Scholarships and fee concessions

- **Merit scholarship:** Students in the top 5,000 KCET ranks receive a 50 percent
  tuition waiver. Ranks between 5,001 and 15,000 receive a 25 percent waiver.
- **Academic excellence scholarship:** Students who score an SGPA above 9.0 in both
  semesters of a year get Rs. 25,000 credited in the following year.
- **Government post-matric scholarship:** SC, ST and Category-1 students from
  Karnataka can claim full fee reimbursement through the State Scholarship Portal
  (SSP). The college scholarship cell at Room A-104 assists with applications.
- **Vidyasiri and D. Devaraj Urs scholarships** are available for eligible OBC and
  minority students.
- **Sports scholarship:** State-level and national-level players receive a 30 percent
  to 100 percent tuition concession, decided by the sports committee.
- **Sibling concession:** If two siblings study at SVIT at the same time, the second
  sibling receives a 15 percent tuition concession.
- **Single girl child concession:** Rs. 15,000 per year.
- **Defence and ex-servicemen wards:** 25 percent tuition concession.

Scholarship applications must be submitted to the scholarship cell by 31 October of
each academic year with income proof and the previous year's marks card.

## Refund policy

If a candidate withdraws before the last date of admission, the full tuition fee is
refunded after deducting Rs. 5,000 as processing charges. Withdrawal after the last
date but before classes begin attracts a deduction of one month's tuition. After
classes begin, the fee is refunded only if the seat is filled by another candidate,
as per AICTE norms. The caution deposit is always fully refundable.
