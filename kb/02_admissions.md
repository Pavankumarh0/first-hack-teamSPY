# Admissions at SVIT

## Undergraduate B.E. admission routes

SVIT fills its 720 undergraduate seats through four routes:

1. **KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test)**  - about 45 percent of seats are filled
   through the Karnataka Examinations Authority counselling process.
2. **COMEDK UGET**  - about 30 percent of seats are filled through the COMEDK
   counselling process.
3. **Management quota**  - about 20 percent of seats are filled directly by the
   college management.
4. **NRI and foreign national quota**  - about 5 percent of seats, five seats per
   branch maximum.

Lateral entry into the second year is available for diploma holders through the
Karnataka Diploma CET (DCET). Twenty percent of the sanctioned intake per branch is
added as lateral entry seats.

## Eligibility

For B.E. admission a candidate must have passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination
with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, plus one of Chemistry, Biology,
Biotechnology, Computer Science or Electronics. The minimum aggregate is 45 percent
for general merit candidates and 40 percent for SC, ST and OBC category candidates
from Karnataka.

For M.Tech admission the candidate must hold a B.E. or B.Tech degree with at least
50 percent aggregate, and admission is through PGCET or a valid GATE score.

## Admission timeline for 2026-27

- Application portal opens: 1 March 2026
- Management quota application deadline: 30 June 2026
- KCET counselling rounds: mid-July to end of August 2026
- COMEDK counselling rounds: July 2026
- Last date for document verification and fee payment: 10 September 2026
- Classes for the first year begin: 22 September 2026

## Documents required at admission

Candidates must bring the original plus two photocopies of each of the following:
SSLC or 10th standard marks card, 10+2 or PUC marks card, transfer certificate,
migration certificate (for non-Karnataka boards), study certificate for the last
seven years, caste and income certificate if claiming reservation, KCET or COMEDK
admission order, Aadhaar card of the student and both parents, and eight recent
passport-size photographs.

## Admission office process for walk-in visitors

Walk-in enquiries are handled at Counter 3 and Counter 4 of the Admission Block
between 9:30 AM and 4:30 PM. A counsellor explains branch availability, the fee
structure and hostel options, and visitors can request a campus tour on the same day.
Management quota seat confirmation requires a non-refundable registration payment of
Rs. 10,000, which is adjusted against the first-year tuition fee.
