# Canteen and Food Facilities at SVIT

## Main canteen

The main canteen is a 9,500 square foot facility between A Block and the library,
with seating for 450 students at a time. It is run by a contractor under the
supervision of a canteen committee that includes the Dean of Student Affairs, two
faculty members and four student representatives. Food rates are approved by the
committee and the approved rate card is displayed at the counter. The canteen is
inspected monthly for hygiene and holds a valid FSSAI licence.

Canteen timings: 8:00 AM to 6:30 PM on working days, 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM on Saturdays.
It is closed on Sundays except during examinations and events.

## Other food outlets on campus

- **Juice and fruit bar** near the indoor sports complex, open 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
  serving fresh juices, fruit bowls, sprouts and sugarcane juice.
- **Cafe Mithra**, a coffee shop on the ground floor of the library building, open
  9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, serving cappuccino, cold coffee, brownies, puffs and sandwiches.
  It has Wi-Fi and 60 seats and is a popular place for group study.
- **Nandini milk parlour** near the main gate, selling milk, curd, buttermilk, lassi
  and ice cream.
- **Snack kiosk in C Block** for quick tea and biscuits between labs.
- **Hostel mess** in the hostel zone, which serves only hostel residents and day
  scholars with meal coupons. Mess details are covered in the hostel information.
- **Food court near the open air theatre** which opens during cultural events and
  fests, with chaat, pav bhaji, Chinese and ice cream stalls.

## Food safety and dietary needs

All outlets serve vegetarian food only, except the hostel mess which serves
non-vegetarian food on Sunday afternoons. Jain food without onion and garlic is
available at the main canteen counter number 2 on request. The canteen displays an
allergen note for items containing peanuts, dairy and gluten. Drinking water with RO
purification is free at all outlets.

A weekly menu is published every Monday on the student portal and on the notice board
outside the canteen. Student feedback is collected through a QR code at each table,
and the canteen committee reviews the feedback every month.
