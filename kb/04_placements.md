# Placements and Training

## Placement information
The college publishes placement statistics on its official website. Figures are reported by year and may change as records are updated. Check https://www.theoxfordengg.org/placement-statistics.htm for the published data; this assistant should not estimate package amounts, placement rates, or employer lists that are not present in the current official report.

## Placement support
For current placement activities, eligibility, or department-specific support, contact the college through its official website at https://www.theoxfordengg.org/ or ask the placement office. This knowledge base does not include a verified current placement calendar or contact person.
