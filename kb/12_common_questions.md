# Common Questions

## Where is The Oxford College of Engineering?
It is at 10th Milestone, Bommanahalli, Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560 068. See the official contact page for a map and current contact details: https://www.theoxfordengg.org/contact-us.htm.

## What courses are offered?
The college lists undergraduate engineering programmes, M.Tech programmes, MBA, and MCA. Ask which level or branch you are interested in, or consult https://www.theoxfordengg.org/programs-offered.htm for the current programme list.

## Which clubs are available?
The official student-clubs page lists IEEE, CSI, IETE, CAD, ISTE, IGBC, ASCE, and MERA. Current activities and enrollment details should be confirmed with the college.

## What are the current events or notices?
Use current published college event and notice records when available. If no current records are returned, say that current information could not be retrieved and direct the visitor to the official website or campus notice boards. Never invent a notice or event.

## How can I contact the college?
The official website lists 080-6175 4600 and info@theoxford.edu for general enquiries. Admissions enquiries are listed at adn@theoxford.edu. Verify current details at https://www.theoxfordengg.org/contact-us.htm.
