# Student Life, Clubs and Support

## Student clubs
The college's official student-clubs page lists IEEE, CSI, IETE, CAD, ISTE, IGBC, ASCE, and MERA. Club activities, meeting times, membership, and contacts may change. See https://www.theoxfordengg.org/student-clubs.htm or ask the relevant department for current details.

## Events and activities
This knowledge base does not hold a verified live events calendar. When asked about upcoming events or activities, use current published event records from the college database if available. If no current records are available, direct the visitor to the official college website or notice boards rather than inventing an event.

## Student support
This knowledge base does not contain verified current support-office contacts or service hours. Contact the college administration using the official contact page for help finding the right office.
