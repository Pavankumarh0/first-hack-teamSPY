# Student Life, Clubs and Support Services at SVIT

## Clubs and student chapters

SVIT has 24 active student clubs. Technical clubs include the Coding Club, Robotics
Club, SAE India collegiate club which builds a go-kart and an off-road vehicle every
year, the Aero Club for drone and RC aircraft projects, the Cybersecurity Club, the
Internet of Things Club, and the Electric Vehicle Club. Professional chapters include
IEEE, CSI, ISTE, SAE, IEI and ASCE student branches.

Cultural and interest clubs include the Music Club, Dance Club, Theatre and Drama
Club, Photography and Film Club, Literary and Debate Club, Fine Arts Club, Kannada
Sangha, the Quiz Club, the Entrepreneurship Cell, the NSS unit, the NCC Army wing with
50 cadets, Youth Red Cross, and the Eco Club which manages campus tree plantation and
waste segregation.

Club membership is free and students may join up to three clubs. Clubs meet on
Wednesdays between 4:45 PM and 6:00 PM, which is kept free of classes for club
activity.

## Annual events

- **Spandana** is the annual inter-collegiate cultural festival held in the last week
  of February over three days, with more than 60 colleges participating and over
  40 events.
- **Tantra** is the annual national-level technical symposium held in March, with
  project exhibitions, hackathons, paper presentations and robotics competitions.
- **Avishkar** is the 36-hour hackathon held in September with a prize pool of
  Rs. 3 lakh.
- **Sports Meet** is held in the first week of January across athletics, cricket,
  football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, chess and kabaddi.
- **Ethnic Day, Freshers Day and the Farewell** are organised each year, along with
  department-level technical days.
- **Graduation day and convocation** are held in November in the main auditorium.

## Achievements

The SVIT Robotics Club team reached the national finals of the e-Yantra robotics
competition in 2024 and 2025. The SAE Baja team finished eleventh nationally in 2025.
The college basketball team won the VTU inter-zone championship in 2024. Three
students represented Karnataka in state-level athletics in 2025.

## Anti-ragging and discipline

Ragging is completely prohibited and is a punishable criminal offence under UGC
regulations. SVIT has an anti-ragging committee headed by the Principal and an
anti-ragging squad that conducts surprise checks in the hostel, canteen, buses and
classrooms. Every student and parent signs an anti-ragging affidavit at the time of
admission. Complaints can be made to the anti-ragging helpline 1800-180-5522, to the
squad members whose numbers are displayed on every notice board, or anonymously
through a drop box in each block. The college has recorded zero substantiated ragging
cases in the last three academic years.

## Internal complaints and grievance redressal

- The Internal Complaints Committee handles complaints of sexual harassment and is
  chaired by Dr. Shobha Rani K, with nine members including an external NGO member.
- The Grievance Redressal Cell in Room A-106 handles academic and administrative
  complaints, and must respond within seven working days.
- The SC and ST Grievance Cell and the Equal Opportunity Cell handle
  discrimination-related complaints.
- An online grievance form is available on the student portal, and the status of each
  complaint is tracked with a ticket number.

## Counselling and wellbeing

A professional student counsellor, Ms. Deepika Jain, a qualified clinical
psychologist, is available on campus from Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, in
Room A-110. Appointments are free and confidential and can be booked by walking in or
through the student portal. Group sessions on exam stress, time management and
homesickness are conducted for first year students every semester. A 24-hour
wellbeing helpline is available on 080-2734 5699.

## Examination and results

Internal assessment contributes 50 marks and the semester-end examination contributes
50 marks for most theory subjects. Students need at least 40 percent in the
semester-end examination and 40 percent overall to pass a subject. Results are
published on results.svit.edu.in within 20 days of the last examination. Revaluation
and photocopy of the answer script can be requested within 10 days of the result, at
Rs. 1,500 and Rs. 500 per subject respectively. Students with backlogs can clear them
through supplementary examinations held after each semester.

## Certifications and value-added courses

The college offers value-added certification courses outside class hours at subsidised
rates, including AWS Cloud Practitioner, Microsoft Azure Fundamentals, Red Hat Linux,
CCNA, PLC and SCADA automation, Revit and STAAD Pro, CATIA, Python for data science,
and embedded systems with ARM. Each course runs 40 to 60 hours and costs between
Rs. 2,500 and Rs. 8,000. NPTEL local chapter courses are free and the college pays the
examination fee for students who complete the course with an above-average score.

## Alumni network

The SVIT Alumni Association has about 18,000 registered members. Alumni meets are held
every December, and alumni contribute to mock interviews, mentorship, the referral
drive during placements, and a student hardship fund that supports about 30 students
per year with fee assistance.

## Uniform and dress code

First and second year students wear the college uniform on Monday, Wednesday and
Friday. Third and fourth year students follow a formal dress code. Lab coats and
safety shoes are compulsory in workshops and laboratories. The uniform set costs about
Rs. 2,400 and is available at the campus uniform counter.
