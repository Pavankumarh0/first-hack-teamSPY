# Hostel Facilities at SVIT

## Hostel blocks at a glance

SVIT has four hostel blocks inside the campus: two for boys and two for girls. Total
hostel capacity is 1,860 students.

## Hostel blocks

- **Kaveri Block (Boys):** 520 beds, four floors, for first and second year students.
- **Tunga Block (Boys):** 480 beds, five floors, for third and fourth year students
  and M.Tech students.
- **Malaprabha Block (Girls):** 500 beds, four floors, for first and second year
  students.
- **Sharavathi Block (Girls):** 360 beds, four floors, for senior girls and
  postgraduate students.

Boys' hostels are 400 metres from the academic block and girls' hostels are 250
metres from the academic block, both within the campus walls.

| Room type | Occupancy | Room rent per year | Mess charges per year | Total per year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Non-AC triple sharing | 3 students | Rs. 42,000 | Rs. 48,000 | Rs. 90,000 |
| Non-AC double sharing | 2 students | Rs. 56,000 | Rs. 48,000 | Rs. 1,04,000 |
| AC double sharing | 2 students | Rs. 78,000 | Rs. 48,000 | Rs. 1,26,000 |
| AC single room | 1 student | Rs. 1,10,000 | Rs. 48,000 | Rs. 1,58,000 |

A refundable hostel caution deposit of Rs. 8,000 is collected at the time of joining.
Hostel fees are paid in two instalments, in July and in December. Single rooms are
limited to 40 across all blocks and are allotted on a first come first served basis
to final year and postgraduate students.

## What the room includes

Every room is furnished with a cot and mattress per student, a study table and chair,
a bookshelf, a personal wardrobe with a lockable door, ceiling fans, LED lighting,
and a power socket set per student. Bedsheets, pillows, buckets and personal items
must be brought by the student. Each floor has a water purifier with a chiller, a
common ironing point, and a solar-heated hot water supply available from 5:30 AM to
9:00 AM.

## Mess and food

The mess operates in two dining halls with a combined seating of 600. Food is fully
vegetarian on weekdays. Non-vegetarian food is served on Sunday afternoons as part of
a special menu. The mess is run by a contractor supervised by a mess committee that
includes six student representatives, and the menu is revised every month based on
student feedback.

Meal timings:
- Breakfast: 7:15 AM to 9:00 AM
- Lunch: 12:30 PM to 2:15 PM
- Evening snacks and tea: 4:45 PM to 5:30 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:15 PM

The menu includes South Indian breakfast items such as idli, dosa, upma, pongal and
poori, North Indian lunch and dinner options with chapati, rice, sambar, two
vegetables, curd and pickle, and fruit or sweet twice a week. Jain and low-oil diet
options are available on request to the mess manager.

## Rules and timings

Hostel entry closing time is 9:00 PM for all students. Permission from the warden is
required to stay out later. Students may go home on weekends after entering their
details in the outing register, with parental consent recorded in the hostel portal.
Study hours are from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, during which the common rooms
and television rooms are closed. Lights out is at 11:00 PM for first year students.

Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco products and any banned substance
inside the hostel results in immediate expulsion from the hostel. Cooking appliances,
immersion heaters and induction stoves are not allowed in rooms. Ragging of any kind
is a criminal offence and leads to expulsion from both hostel and college.

## Hostel amenities

Each block has a reading room, an indoor games room with table tennis and carrom, a
television lounge with DTH connection, Wi-Fi at 100 Mbps per block, a laundry room
with washing machines (Rs. 30 per wash using a prepaid card), an RO drinking water
plant, a 24-hour power backup through diesel generators, CCTV cameras in all corridors
and entrances, biometric entry at the main gate, and a resident warden on each floor.

Girls' hostels have an additional layer of security with women security staff at the
entrance round the clock and a separate visitors' room where parents can meet their
daughters between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

## Medical support

A primary health centre with two beds, a resident nurse available 24 hours, and a
visiting doctor from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM daily is located between the two boys' blocks.
An ambulance is stationed on campus at all times. The nearest multi-speciality
hospital is Harohalli Government Hospital at 4 km and Sapthagiri Hospital at 11 km.

Day scholars can use a separate rest lounge near the canteen, lockers available for
Rs. 500 per year, and the mess on a per-meal coupon basis at Rs. 60 for lunch.
