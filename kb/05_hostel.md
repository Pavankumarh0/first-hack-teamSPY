# Hostel Facilities

## Hostel information
The official college hostel page describes residential facilities for students, with the girls' hostel on campus and boys' hostel accommodation outside the campus. The page also describes wardens, security, mess facilities, solar hot water, and RO drinking water. Details such as availability, fees, rules, and capacity can change; confirm them directly at https://www.theoxfordengg.org/hostels.htm.

## Hostel enquiries
This knowledge base does not contain verified current room availability, hostel fees, application dates, or a hostel phone number. Contact the college using the official contact page before making arrangements.
