The problem

Every visitor asks the same
fifty questions.

On admission day a parent stops the nearest student and asks about hostel rooms, the fee for the management seat, last year's placements, which bus comes from Electronic City, whether the canteen is vegetarian, where to park the car. The answers are scattered across a brochure, a notice board and whoever happens to be standing there. Campus Mithra puts all of it in one voice that never guesses.

🏫

Grounded, not guessing

Retrieval augmented generation over the college's own knowledge base. Gemma only rewrites what the documents already say.

🚫

Refuses politely

Ask about the weather or another college and it says the answer is not in the knowledge base, instead of inventing one.

🎤

Speaks and listens

A microphone, a speaker and a 2.8 inch screen on an ESP32, so a visitor just talks to it at the admission desk.

How a question travels

From a spoken word to a
sourced answer.

01

Listen

The earphone mic on the ESP32 captures the question and streams it to the host.

02

Embed

MiniLM turns the question into a 384 dimension vector in a few milliseconds.

03

Retrieve

Cosine similarity ranks every 120 word chunk and keeps the best three.

04

Gate

Three guards decide whether the question belongs here at all. Two of them refuse before the model is ever called.

05

Answer

Gemma composes two to five spoken sentences at temperature 0.2, then the TFT prints it and the speaker says it.
