# Sri Venkateshwara Institute of Technology (SVIT)  - College Overview

Sri Venkateshwara Institute of Technology, commonly called SVIT, is an autonomous
engineering college located at Kanakapura Main Road, Harohalli, Bengaluru South,
Karnataka 562112. The college was established in 1998 by the Sri Venkateshwara
Education Trust.

SVIT is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and is
approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. The
institution received autonomous status in 2016 and is accredited by NAAC with an
A+ grade (CGPA 3.42) valid until 2029. Six undergraduate programmes are accredited
by NBA under Tier-1 until June 2027.

The campus spreads across 42 acres with a built-up area of about 6.2 lakh square feet.
Total student strength is approximately 4,800, with 3,950 undergraduate students,
620 postgraduate students and 230 research scholars. The student-to-faculty ratio is
17:1.

## Departments

SVIT has nine academic departments: Computer Science and Engineering, Information
Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Electronics
and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Mechanical
Engineering, Civil Engineering, Mathematics, and Physics and Chemistry (basic
sciences).

## Academic calendar

The odd semester runs from August to December and the even semester from February to
June. There are two internal assessment tests per semester plus one preparatory
examination. Minimum attendance of 75 percent is mandatory to be eligible to write
the semester-end examination.
