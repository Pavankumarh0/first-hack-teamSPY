# Campus Infrastructure at SVIT

The SVIT campus covers 42 acres and is divided into the academic zone, the hostel
zone, the sports zone and the green belt. All buildings are connected by covered
walkways.

## Academic buildings

- **Main Academic Block (A Block):** Five floors with 48 classrooms, all fitted with
  smart boards, projectors and air conditioning in the first three floors.
- **B Block:** Houses Computer Science, Information Science and AIML departments with
  14 computer laboratories.
- **C Block:** Houses Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical and Civil departments along
  with their laboratories and workshops.
- **Research and Innovation Block (D Block):** Three floors with research centres,
  the incubation centre, and postgraduate project labs.
- **Administrative Block:** Principal's office, accounts, examination section,
  admission block and the placement cell.
- **Seminar halls:** Four halls with capacities of 120, 150, 200 and 250 seats.
- **Auditorium:** The Sir M. Visvesvaraya Auditorium seats 1,200 people and has a
  fully air-conditioned interior with professional sound and lighting.
- **Open air theatre:** Capacity of 800, used for cultural events.

## Classrooms

The college has 48 classrooms and 12 tutorial rooms. Standard classroom capacity is
72 students. Every classroom has a smart interactive panel, a ceiling-mounted
projector, a document camera, Wi-Fi coverage, and ergonomic bench seating. Eighteen
classrooms are equipped for lecture recording, and recorded lectures are published on
the student learning portal within 24 hours.

## Laboratories

There are 52 laboratories in total. Notable facilities include:

- Four programming labs with 70 systems each, running Intel Core i5 13th generation
  with 16 GB RAM
- A GPU lab with 12 workstations having NVIDIA RTX 4080 cards, used for AI and machine
  learning projects
- Internet of Things and Embedded Systems lab with ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi and
  STM32 development kits, logic analysers and oscilloscopes
- VLSI design lab with Cadence and Synopsys licensed tools
- Robotics and automation lab with six-axis industrial robot arms and PLC trainers
- CAD and CAM centre with 45 licensed seats of AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA and ANSYS
- Thermal engineering, fluid mechanics and strength of materials labs
- Environmental engineering, concrete testing and surveying labs with total stations
  and drone-based survey equipment
- Communication systems lab with spectrum analysers and antenna trainer kits
- Three physics and chemistry labs for first year students

## Library

The Central Library is in a dedicated three-floor building called the Knowledge
Centre, with a seating capacity of 450 and a carpet area of 22,000 square feet.

- Total books: 68,400 volumes across 9,200 titles
- Journals: 142 print journals and access to IEEE Xplore, Springer Link, ScienceDirect,
  ASME, ASCE, J-Gate and DELNET digital libraries
- NPTEL and NDLI video course access from 30 dedicated terminals
- A digital library section with 60 computers
- A separate reference and rare books section
- A reading hall that remains open from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM during examinations
- Normal library hours: 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM on working days, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on
  Saturdays
- Students may borrow four books for 14 days; postgraduate students may borrow six
  books for 21 days. A fine of Rs. 2 per day applies for late returns.
- Librarian: Dr. Harini Prasad, phone 080-2734 5625

## Internet and IT

The campus has a 1 Gbps leased line with a 300 Mbps backup link, and campus-wide
Wi-Fi through 310 access points. Students get individual login credentials with 25 GB
of daily data. The data centre hosts the ERP, learning management system and a private
cloud with 240 virtual machines for lab use.

## Power and water

The campus has a 1,250 kVA transformer supply with three diesel generators totalling
800 kVA for backup, so classes and labs continue uninterrupted. A 420 kW rooftop solar
plant supplies about 32 percent of the daytime load. Water comes from four borewells
and a Cauvery supply connection, with a 4.5 lakh litre storage capacity and a sewage
treatment plant of 250 kilolitres per day whose treated water is used for landscaping.
