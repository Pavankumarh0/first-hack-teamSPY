# Transportation and Bus Routes at SVIT

## College bus fleet

SVIT operates a fleet of 34 buses covering 28 routes across Bengaluru and nearby
towns. The fleet includes 26 full-size 54-seater buses, six 32-seater mini buses for
narrow interior routes, and two accessible buses with wheelchair lifts. All buses
have GPS tracking, a speed governor limited to 60 km per hour, a first aid kit, a fire
extinguisher, and CCTV inside. Parents and students can track their bus live on the
SVIT Transport mobile app using the route number.

## Annual bus fees by distance slab

| Distance from campus | Annual bus fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 10 km | Rs. 18,000 |
| 10 km to 20 km | Rs. 24,000 |
| 20 km to 30 km | Rs. 30,000 |
| 30 km to 45 km | Rs. 36,000 |
| Above 45 km | Rs. 42,000 |

The bus fee is paid in two instalments along with the tuition fee. A one-way-only
concession of 40 percent is available for students who use the bus only in the
morning. Bus passes are issued as RFID cards, and the card must be tapped while
boarding and alighting.

## Return timings

Buses leave the campus at 4:45 PM on regular working days and at 1:30 PM on Saturdays.
A late bus on routes R-01, R-02, R-03, R-04 and R-12 leaves the campus at 6:15 PM for
students who stay back for placement training, laboratory work or library study.

## Public transport options

The campus is on Kanakapura Main Road, 32 km from Bengaluru Majestic bus station.
BMTC routes 215-G, 215-K and V-356 pass the campus gate, and the frequency is about
every 20 minutes. The nearest Namma Metro station is Thalaghattapura on the Green
Line, 18 km away, and the college runs a shuttle between Thalaghattapura metro
station and the campus at 7:30 AM, 8:15 AM, 4:50 PM and 6:20 PM for Rs. 8,000 per
year. The nearest railway station is Bengaluru City Junction at 34 km and Kempegowda
International Airport is 68 km away.

## Transport office

The transport office is located next to the main gate security block. Transport
Officer: Mr. Mahesh Gowda, phone 080-2734 5618 and 98807 44556. Office hours are
8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on working days. Route changes, duplicate bus passes (Rs. 200) and
stop change requests are handled here. A stop can be changed twice a year without
charge if a bus on that route has seats available.

## Safety

Every bus has a staff escort assigned, and women escorts travel on routes that carry
girl students on early morning or late evening trips. Drivers undergo an annual
medical check and a defensive driving refresher course. A breath analyser check is
done for all drivers before the morning trip. Complaints about driving or conduct can
be reported on the transport helpline 1800-425-7789, which is monitored by the Dean of
Student Affairs.
