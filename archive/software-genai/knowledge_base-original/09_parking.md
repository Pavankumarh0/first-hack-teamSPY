# Parking Facilities at SVIT

The campus has four designated parking zones with a total capacity of 1,420 two
wheelers and 310 four wheelers. All parking areas are surfaced, lit at night, covered
by 28 CCTV cameras, and patrolled by security staff.

## Parking zones

- **Zone P1  - Student two-wheeler parking:** Located behind B Block, this is a covered
  shed with capacity for 900 two wheelers. Open from 7:00 AM to 7:30 PM.
- **Zone P2  - Student two-wheeler overflow and hostel parking:** Near the Kaveri
  hostel block, capacity 520 two wheelers, accessible 24 hours for hostel residents.
- **Zone P3  - Staff and faculty parking:** Beside the Administrative Block, capacity
  180 four wheelers and 120 two wheelers, reserved with name-marked bays for
  department heads.
- **Zone P4  - Visitor and parent parking:** Immediately inside the main gate to the
  left, capacity 130 four wheelers and open visitor two-wheeler space. This is the
  zone parents should use when visiting for admission enquiries or events.

An additional temporary parking ground near the open air theatre is opened during
convocation, the annual cultural festival and placement drives, and can hold about
250 more four wheelers.

## Parking charges and passes

Parking inside the campus is free for students, staff and visitors. However, every
student vehicle must be registered with the security office and display a college
parking sticker on the windscreen or headlight.

Vehicle registration requires a copy of the vehicle registration certificate, a valid
driving licence of the student, a valid insurance certificate, a pollution under
control certificate, and a consent letter signed by a parent for first and second year
students. The sticker is issued free within two working days from the security office
near the main gate. A duplicate sticker costs Rs. 100.

## Rules

- Helmets are compulsory for two-wheeler riders and pillion riders inside and outside
  the campus. Security staff at the gate stop riders without helmets.
- The campus speed limit is 20 km per hour.
- Four wheelers driven by students are allowed only with written permission from the
  Dean of Student Affairs, and must be parked in Zone P1 extension.
- Vehicles without a valid parking sticker are wheel-clamped, and release requires a
  written undertaking from the student plus a Rs. 500 fine on the second offence.
- Parking in fire lanes, in front of ramps, near the water treatment plant or on
  internal roads is not allowed. Such vehicles are towed to Zone P2.
- Modified silencers and vehicles that fail the noise check are barred from the campus.
- The college is not responsible for theft or damage to vehicles, though all incidents
  are investigated with CCTV footage support.

## Electric vehicle charging

Zone P1 has eight two-wheeler EV charging points and Zone P4 has four four-wheeler EV
charging points of 7.4 kW each. Charging is billed at Rs. 9 per unit through a prepaid
card obtained from the accounts office.
