# Placements and Training at SVIT

## Placement statistics for the 2024-25 batch

Out of 892 eligible students, 806 were placed, giving a placement rate of 90.4
percent. A total of 1,142 offers were made because many students received multiple
offers. 184 companies visited the campus during the placement season.

- Highest package: Rs. 46 lakh per annum (offered by Google to a CSE student)
- Second highest package: Rs. 38 lakh per annum (Atlassian)
- Average package across all branches: Rs. 7.4 lakh per annum
- Median package: Rs. 6.2 lakh per annum
- Lowest package: Rs. 3.2 lakh per annum
- Number of students with offers above Rs. 10 lakh per annum: 148
- Number of students who received internship stipends above Rs. 50,000 per month: 31

## Training and Placement Cell

The Training and Placement Cell is located on the ground floor of the Administrative
Block, Rooms G-12 to G-18. It is headed by Mr. Anand Kulkarni, Training and Placement
Officer, supported by three placement coordinators and two student coordinators per
department.

Facilities include a 120-seat pre-placement talk auditorium, eight group discussion
rooms, four video-interview booths with dedicated leased-line internet, and an
aptitude lab with 60 systems.

## Training programmes

- Soft skills and communication training begins in the third semester and runs for
  60 hours across two semesters.
- Aptitude and logical reasoning training of 120 hours is conducted in the fifth and
  sixth semesters by external trainers.
- Technical training includes Data Structures and Algorithms bootcamps, full-stack
  development tracks, cloud certification preparation for AWS and Azure, and
  competitive programming sessions every Saturday.
- Mock interviews and mock group discussions with industry professionals are held in
  the sixth and seventh semesters.
- Students must clear an internal eligibility test with 60 percent to sit for
  placement drives, and must have no active backlogs for most core companies.

## Internships

A mandatory eight-week internship is part of the curriculum after the sixth semester.
In 2025, 714 students completed internships, of which 268 were paid internships. 142
students received pre-placement offers from their internship companies.

## Higher studies and entrepreneurship

In the 2024-25 batch, 64 students went for higher studies, mostly to universities in
the United States, Germany and Australia, and 11 students started their own ventures
with support from the SVIT Innovation and Incubation Centre, which offers seed
funding up to Rs. 5 lakh and free office space for two years.
