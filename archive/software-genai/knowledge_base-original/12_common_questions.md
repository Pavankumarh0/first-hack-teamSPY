# Frequently Asked Questions by Visiting Parents and Students

**Is SVIT affiliated and approved?**
Yes. SVIT is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University, approved by AICTE,
accredited by NAAC with an A+ grade, and six undergraduate programmes are NBA
accredited.

**How far is the college from the city?**
The campus is on Kanakapura Main Road at Harohalli, about 32 km from Majestic in
central Bengaluru. The drive takes 60 to 90 minutes depending on traffic. College
buses cover 28 routes across the city.

**Is the hostel compulsory?**
No. The hostel is optional. Students who live within reasonable travel distance
usually take the college bus as day scholars.

**Can parents visit their child in the hostel?**
Yes. Parents can meet their child in the hostel visitors room between 9:00 AM and
6:00 PM after entering their details in the visitor register at the hostel office.

**What is the total first-year cost for a day scholar under KCET government quota?**
Tuition is Rs. 68,000, plus a one-time admission fee of Rs. 12,000, university and
examination fee Rs. 9,500, laboratory fee Rs. 8,000, library fee Rs. 3,000, activity
fee Rs. 4,500, insurance Rs. 1,200 and a refundable caution deposit of Rs. 10,000.
Bus fees depend on the distance slab.

**Does the college help with education loans?**
Yes. The accounts office provides the fee structure letter, bonafide certificate and
admission confirmation needed for loan processing, and the college has tie-ups with
State Bank of India, Canara Bank and HDFC Bank.

**What is the attendance requirement?**
A minimum of 75 percent attendance in each subject is mandatory to write the
semester-end examination. Parents receive attendance updates by SMS and email after
every internal test.

**Are laptops required?**
A personal laptop is recommended from the third semester onward but is not compulsory.
All laboratories have sufficient systems, and the computer labs are open for student
use until 6:30 PM.

**Does the college allow students to bring vehicles?**
Two wheelers are allowed with a parking sticker issued by the security office, and
helmets are compulsory. Students need written permission from the Dean of Student
Affairs to bring a four wheeler.

**How safe is the campus for girl students?**
The campus has 24-hour security, 310 CCTV cameras, biometric hostel entry, women
security staff at the girls hostel blocks, women escorts on early and late bus trips,
an Internal Complaints Committee, and a 24-hour wellbeing helpline.

**What is the medical facility on campus?**
A primary health centre with a resident nurse available 24 hours, a visiting doctor
daily from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM, and an ambulance stationed on campus. Harohalli
Government Hospital is 4 km away and Sapthagiri Hospital is 11 km away.

**Can I see the campus before taking admission?**
Yes. Free guided campus tours start at the Admission Block reception every working day
at 10:30 AM and 3:00 PM. No appointment is needed.

**Whom should I contact for admission?**
Call the admission helpline on 080-2734 5611 or the toll free number 1800-425-7788,
or email admissions@svit.edu.in. Walk-in enquiries are handled at Counters 3 and 4 of
the Admission Block between 9:30 AM and 4:30 PM.

**Does the college have an AI assistant for visitors?**
Yes. Campus Mithra is the AI campus assistant deployed at the Admission Block
reception. Visitors can ask it questions by voice and it answers using the official
college information only.
